Schedule & Course

We are designing an updated, scenic 5K route along the Manistique boardwalk that reduces highway exposure and ends at Folk Fest in downtown Manistique.

  • Race Date & Start Time:
    Saturday, July 10, 2027 @ 9:00 AM
  • Start & Finish Location:
    TBD (Final route details coming soon)
  • Check-In & Packet Pick-Up:
    • Friday, July 9: 5:00 - 7:00 PM at Manistique High School
    • Saturday, July 10: 7:30 - 8:30 AM at the Start Line
  • Post-Race Refreshments:
    Light snacks and water will be provided at the finish line.
